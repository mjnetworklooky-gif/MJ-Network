## 🎬 About

**MJ Network [MJN]** is a fan-made Roblox project dedicated to recreating and celebrating Michael Jackson's most iconic performances, moments, stages, and legacy.

Founded by **@Looky in March 2026**, MJN combines Roblox development, stage design, lighting, visuals, and performance recreation to bring legendary moments back to life.

## 🕺 What We Do

- 🎤 Recreate iconic Michael Jackson performances
- 🎬 Rebuild legendary stages and moments in Roblox
- 💡 Create custom lighting and visual effects
- 🏟️ Recreate famous concerts and appearances
- 👑 Celebrate Michael Jackson's legacy
